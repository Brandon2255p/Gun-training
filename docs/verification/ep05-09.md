## Remaining doubts (not fixed, outside these files)

1. **Pistol hammer is static.** `Handgun.tsx` draws the hammer in one fixed, rearward-leaning pose and has no decock action. Three places are affected:
   - ep06 Step 5, "lower the hammer under control": the hammer is highlighted but never moves.
   - ep09 "whether the hammer is back or forward": the drawing always shows it back.
   - ep08 rule 9 and ep09: the drawing cannot show a hammer-forward condition.

   The narration is correct. A hammer state or decock action for the pistol would let the drawing match.
2. **Ejector-rod callout when the cylinder is open.** In `Revolver.tsx`, the side-view ejector rod fades out as the cylinder opens, but its callout anchor stays at the side-view position under the barrel. In ep07 step 5, the "Ejector rod" label therefore points at an empty spot, while the end-on centre pin is coloured. Worth a look in the stills.
3. **ep08 length and scope.** The lint estimate is about 6.8 minutes, above the 3–6 minute target, and the episode covers two lists (general and range rules). Splitting it would need a new episode and series changes, so I left it.
4. **Revolver step numbering.** On p. 14 the revolver is finished at Step 3 ("no further steps required"), but the p. 16 photos number the revolver procedure 1–5. ep07 follows the p. 16 photo numbering and also quotes p. 14's "no further steps are required". Both come from the book, and nothing in the script depends on the difference.
5. Not checked: rendered stills. CLAUDE.md requires `pnpm prepare-assets`, `pnpm check` and `pnpm render --stills ep05…ep09` before rendering video, and the task excluded running the pipeline. These are still needed to confirm the longer ep09 numbered bullets and quiz options fit on screen.
