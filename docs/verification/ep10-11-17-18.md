## Remaining doubts

- ep17 loading diagram: the magazine visibly slides out during the first line. This setup isn't a book step. The narration now says
  so, and the drawing is still sliding in at that moment. A "start with magazine out" scene option would remove it completely, but that is a code change and out of scope.
- ep17 unloading: the drawing can't show the magazine well (it isn't a drawing part), and the pistol drawing's hammer doesn't move.
  So "check… down the magazine well" and "lower the hammer under control" are only highlighted or narrated, not animated.
- ep17 figure "Loading: steps 7 to 9": the crop shows photo 7, but the narration only covers steps 8 and 9 (step 7 was taught in the diagram just before).
- Exam p. 78 asks for "the nine correct steps for loading either a semi-automatic pistol or a revolver", but the book's
  right-handed revolver loading has eight steps. Neither episode depends on this mismatch: ep17 cites the exam only for the
  pistol's nine steps, and ep18 doesn't cite it.
- Narration in ep18 uses semicolons in two recap texts. Lint accepts them. It's a style choice for TTS.
