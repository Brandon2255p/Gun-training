All five definitions, causes and corrective actions match p. 56.
- Misfire: a faulty or dead primer, or a light or soft strike. For a pistol, Tap, Rack, Assess. For a revolver, squeeze the trigger again.

- Squib load: from powder contamination or too little powder. Take the firearm to a gunsmith.
- Stove-pipe jam: covered in full.
